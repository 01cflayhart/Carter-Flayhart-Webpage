Tennis: Court Current

The official rankings tell one story. I wanted to look more closely at recent form, the quality of a player’s opponents, and how results differ by surface.

Court Current is my attempt at that. ATP and WTA are rated separately, and each player has a notebook showing the results behind their ranking. Each dashboard also has a searchable published-ranking table: ATP standings and WTA standings via TennisONE, checked daily.

A note on the data

This is an ongoing project, not an official ranking. Coverage is incomplete, and the model is provisional. The dashboards include the latest accepted results, source information, methods, and known gaps.
