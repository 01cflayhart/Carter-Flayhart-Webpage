Prediction history & daily review

Methods, sources & what’s missing

The baseline estimates a threshold’s hit probability from dated, current-season game observations with recency weighting and conservative smoothing. Verified pregame matchup and opportunity multipliers adjust those observations; unknown injuries or player identities block the pick instead of becoming guessed advantages.

Value requires a positive edge over the quoted implied probability. Consistency picks require at least eight current-season games, a 90% observed hit rate and a projected likelihood of at least 85%; these provisional thresholds cannot establish near-100% reliability.

Daily review scores frozen predictions, computes calibration error and proposes changes for review. It does not rewrite past picks or tune a model to yesterday’s winners. Licensed odds and verified identity, injury, matchup and settlement feeds must be connected before live predictions can appear.

Open NFL statistics connection: nflverse (CC BY 4.0). Odds adapter: The Odds API, signup required; coverage and free quota are limited.
